Tourbook

The booking deck for music producers and the bands they play in, manage or produce.

Early accessiPhoneAndroidComputer

Tourbook keeps your shows, studio sessions and rehearsals in one calendar, with your acts, the people you work with, your backline, the venues and studios you play, and your songs and setlists. On the day it gives the band a day sheet, tunes your guitar, counts you in and times your set. Afterwards it settles the night, splits the money and makes the invoice.

Open Tourbook

Free to use while it's new.

Tourbook is in early access: every part of it is free for now, with nothing to sign up for. Later, the Audiophile role (for going to shows and keeping track of the bands you like) stays free for good, for your own personal use. The working roles get 30 days free on each device, then a licence of 20 US dollars once, for up to 3 of your devices. No subscription. A desktop app for Windows, Mac and Linux is on the way; until then, Tourbook installs from the browser on a phone or a computer.

Made for the way you work

The first time it opens, Tourbook asks who's using it: producer, manager, band, solo artist, scout or audiophile, or Jack of Trades for a bit of all of it. Each gets its own menu and pages, and you can change any time.

What it does

Bookings

Shows, studio sessions and rehearsals on one calendar, by month, week or day. Open one for everything about it.

Acts and people

The bands and artists you work with, grouped by genre, with press kits and riders; band members, session players, crew, promoters and agents.

Songs and setlists

Keys, tempos and lengths; split sheets for the writers; setlists that add up to your slot.

Studio log

A take log for recording: add a take, mark the keepers, note what to fix.

Backline

Instruments, amps, mics and cables with serial numbers and values; stage packs for the gigs you do often.

Venues

Capacity, stage, PA, curfew, load-in and the house rules, on every show there, with a map.

Day sheets

Everything the band needs on the day, shared as a link, with no money on it.

Money

What you've earned and what's unpaid, deposits due, settlements, each person's share, and invoices.

Tools

A tuner, a metronome, tap tempo with delay times, a sound level meter and a set timer.

Yours, and in sync

No account: your information stays on your own phone or computer, and you can save a backup any time. Coming soon: connect your own Google Drive to keep your phone and computer the same, with a dated copy each day.

Get started

  1. Open Tourbook at tourbook.crowmade.app on your phone or computer.
  2. Put it on your home screen so it opens like a normal app, even with no signal. iPhone: Safari's Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Android and computers: tap Install at the top of the page.
  3. Pick your role, then tap ? at the top of any page for a walkthrough of it, box by box.
