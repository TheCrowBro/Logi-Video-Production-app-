Vdex

The production deck for people who film things for a living.

Available nowiPhoneAndroidWindowsMacLinux

Vdex keeps your shoots, your crew, your camera gear, the places you film and your insurance in one place. On the day of a shoot it clocks your hours, prints your call sheet and tells you when the sun sets. Afterwards it makes the quote and the invoice.

Buy a licence

Works on iPhone, Android and computers. Free to try for 30 days, then 20 US dollars once plus any tax where you live, for up to 3 of your devices. With tax that is 24.00 US dollars in the UK, 22.00 in Australia, and 23.40 to 25.40 in the EU, depending on the country (every country's price). No subscription, and no account for your work: your information stays on your own device unless you choose to copy it somewhere.

You are buying a licence to use Vdex, not ownership of a copy. It can be cancelled in the cases the terms describe. At checkout you can pay in US dollars, or in your own currency at Stripe's exchange rate (2 to 4% more).

What it does

Bookings

A calendar of shoots. Tap a day, then a shoot, to see everything about it: the client, the address, the crew and the kit you're taking.

Crew, equipment, venues and insurance

The people you work with, everything you own with serial numbers and where each item is, the places you film with their rules and parking, and when your policies run out.

The shoot-day clock

Tap Start work, Break and Stop work, and the hours are counted for you.

Call sheets and a crew link

The call sheet is already filled in from the booking: add the schedule, then email it or print it. Or send your cast and crew a link to the schedule, location and shot list, with no prices in it.

Stripboard

Pick the script and every scene becomes a colour-coded strip with its cast and page count. Drag the strips into shooting days.

Budget, quotes and invoices

A private budget for each shoot, a price list that starts from typical freelance rates where you work, and quotes and invoices with Pay buttons for the client.

Field tools

Sunrise and sunset, a white balance reader, a noise meter and a range finder.

Timecode notes

Shoot notes stamped with the camera's timecode, with a start-to-finish guide on a practice log.

Safe and in sync

Lock it with a PIN, fingerprint or face. With a licence, connect Google Drive, iCloud, Dropbox or OneDrive to share your information between your phone and computer, with a daily backup.

Get started

  1. Open Vdex at vdex.crowmade.app, or download the desktop app from the download page.
  2. Put it on your home screen so it works like a normal app, even with no signal. iPhone: Safari's Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Android and computers: tap Install at the top of the page.
  3. Tap ? at the top of any page for a walkthrough of that page, box by box.
