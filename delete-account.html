Delete your Vdex licence records

For Vdex, made by Crowmade (Laurence Simmonds, known online as TheCrowBro). Vdex has no account for your work: everything you type stays on your own device. What our licence server holds about you is the record below, and this page is how to have it deleted. We also have your order in our Stripe account, a note of any free licence we gave you, and emails you sent us; ask and we delete what the law lets us.

How to ask

  1. In the app (quickest): open Vdex, go to Settings → General → About & Licence, and tap Delete my licence records while you are signed in (on the website and in the Android and iPhone apps). It is done at once and you are signed out there. A computer that still has your licence code puts itself back at its next weekly check: use Release this computer there first.
  2. By email: write to support@crowmade.app from the email address your licence belongs to, with the subject "Delete my licence records". We delete the record within 30 days and reply when it is done. This is the way if you only use the desktop app, which has no such button.

What is deleted

  • The email address (or App Store purchase) your licence is linked to for signing in.
  • The list of devices on your licence, with each one's random installation number.
  • Every earlier sign-in: they stop working, and you can sign in again afresh if you want.

What is kept, and for how long

  • A record that a licence was cancelled (for example after a refund), so it cannot be used again, and the count of recent device moves. These hold no name and no email address.
  • A scrambled marker, kept for up to 400 days, that makes older sign-ins stay invalid. It cannot be turned back into your email address.
  • Stripe, the seller named on your receipt and Apple keep their own records of a purchase, under their own policies. We cannot delete those; ask them.

Your licence itself is not cancelled by this. If you sign in again, restore an App Store purchase, or use your licence code on a computer, the record is made afresh.

Your work on your own devices

The bookings, crew, kit and everything else you typed into Vdex never reach us. They live on your device, and in the cloud storage you linked yourself. To remove them, use Settings → Storage, sync & backups → Erase all data on this device before you uninstall. Uninstalling alone can leave them on the device. Then delete Vdex's copy and its backups in your own Google Drive or other storage (section 17 of the terms says how for each).

More detail is in the privacy policy.
