First time only: make your keys

Do this once, before selling. It makes three keys:

  1. Your key (vdex-licence-key-1.json): makes free codes in this page.
  2. The shop key (vdex-licence-key-2-shop.json): Cloudflare uses it to make codes for paying customers. You paste it into Cloudflare once.
  3. A spare (vdex-licence-key-9-spare.json): replaces your key if it is ever lost or leaked. Keep it somewhere else, such as a USB stick in a drawer.
