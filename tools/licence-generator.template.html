Vdex licence generator

For the owner only. Keep this file, your key files and your passphrase on your own computer. Making keys and codes works with no internet; looking up, clearing and cancelling codes, and deleting someone's records, talks to vdex.crowmade.app.

This browser cannot sign licence codes. Open this file in an up-to-date Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

3. Look up, clear or cancel a code

Paste a code, or its ref from the ledger (like 2:3735928559; codes bought in the shop start with 2:, codes you made with 1:). "Link for website sign-in" needs the whole code: it lets the person sign in on the website and in the Android app with the email address in it (codes you make here are linked by themselves when you are online).

4. Delete what the licence server holds about someone

For someone who asks you to delete their licence records and cannot press "Delete my licence records" in Vdex themselves. It does the same for the email address you type: the address's links to its licences, a sign-in code waiting for it, and the list of devices on those licences are deleted, and every sign-in made with the address stops working. The licence itself is not cancelled. This needs the deletion secret you set in Cloudflare (FORGET_SECRET, see docs/selling-desktop.md), not your key.

First time only: make your keys

Do this once, before selling. It makes three keys:

  1. Your key (vdex-licence-key-1.json): makes free codes in this page.
  2. The shop key (vdex-licence-key-2-shop.json): Cloudflare uses it to make codes for paying customers. You paste it into Cloudflare once.
  3. A spare (vdex-licence-key-9-spare.json): replaces your key if it is ever lost or leaked. Keep it somewhere else, such as a USB stick in a drawer.

All three are locked with the passphrase below. Keep it in your password manager: without it the key files are useless, to you as well.
