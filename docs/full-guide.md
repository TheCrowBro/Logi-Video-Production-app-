# Vdex: the complete guide

This is the long version. The short one is the [README](../README.md).

**Vdex is a production deck for freelance videographers and small video businesses.** Bookings, crew, kit, venues, insurance, a shoot-day clock, timecode notes, pre- and post-production checklists and a few field tools, all in one app that lives on your phone and works on your computer too.

- **No subscription, and no server for your work.** A 30-day free trial, then a one-time licence (you sign in with its email address on the website). Your data stays on your device and only goes where *you* send it (your own Google Drive, iCloud, Dropbox, OneDrive or server).
- **Works offline** once set up, which matters on location. A licence is checked online at least once every 90 days.
- **Locks with a PIN, or your fingerprint or face,** and keeps your work and your sign-ins encrypted whenever it is locked, in the background or closed. Helps you keep your data safe: automatic daily backups in your cloud storage, encrypted backup files, and a reminder when there's no recent copy.
- **Phone and computer share one database** once you link a cloud storage.
- **On a computer, one click installs it:** there is an installer for Windows, Mac and Linux, or use it in any browser.

## iPhone guide

### Install it on your iPhone

There are two ways. **The Vdex app on the App Store** (search for Vdex, or follow the link on vdex.crowmade.app) is the same Vdex with what only an app can do on an iPhone: break alerts that arrive with the app closed, a "Vdex bookings" calendar in the Calendar app, backups and spreadsheets saved through the Files app, scripts and shot lists handed to it from Mail or Files, printing through the phone's print sheet, and the licence bought in the app. Its Install button does not show, because it is installed already.

Or, with no app at all, install the website from the browser:

1. Open the Vdex address in **Safari**. Chrome, Edge and Firefox can add it to the Home Screen too (iOS 16.4 or later).
2. Tap **Share**: in Safari the square with an arrow at the bottom of the screen, in the other browsers beside the address or in the browser's menu.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. From now on, open Vdex from its Home Screen icon.

Tapping **Install Vdex** in Vdex's own menu (**☰** at the top right) shows these same steps.

Why install it rather than keep it in a Safari tab:

- **It keeps your data.** Safari may clear a website's saved data after about a week of not using it. The installed app is kept apart from Safari and doesn't lose data that way.
- **Notifications only work in the installed app.** Safari tabs can't show them. Camera, microphone and location work in the installed app on iOS 16.4 or later.
- **It opens full-screen and works offline.**

**One thing to know:** the installed app and the Safari tab are two separate copies with separate data. If you entered bookings in Safari before installing, export a backup there (with a licence; Settings → *Storage, sync & backups* → **Export backup** (with a licence)) and import it in the installed app, or link the same cloud storage on both and they merge.

### Let Vdex use the camera, microphone and location

iPhone asks the first time a tool needs something. Tap **Allow**.

| It asks for | Used by |
|---|---|
| Camera | Scanning the camera's timecode, reading white balance, scanning a link code |
| Microphone | The noise meter |
| Location | Sun, golden hour and Polaris, and the sunrise and sunset on call sheets |
| Motion & Orientation | Aiming at the sun or Polaris, and the tilt range finder |
| Notifications | "Break's up" alerts from the shoot-day clock |

If you tapped **Don't Allow** by mistake: the camera and microphone are asked for again next time. For location, open iPhone **Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Safari Websites** (installed web apps usually follow that setting). For notifications, open **Settings → Notifications**, find Vdex and switch it on, then turn the toggle on again inside Vdex (Settings → *Work & billing* → *Hours & breaks*).

### The first time you open it

1. **Set a PIN.** Vdex asks for one before anything else (4 to 20 digits) and locks with it from then on. A forgotten PIN cannot be recovered, so keep a cloud storage linked or a backup current. **The set-up walkthrough** then starts by itself: a look around the deck, your name, business, phone, email and website (they go on your call sheets, quotes, invoices and contact card), your calendar, and two sample people on the Crew page with how to remove them. Replay it any time from the menu (**☰** at the top right): **Text size and theme**, then *Take the tour again*. **Tutorial for this page**, in the same menu, is the tutorial of the page you're on.
2. **Want to see it full of data first?** Settings → *Storage, sync & backups* → **Load sample data** fills it with example bookings, crew, kit and venues. It replaces what's there, on your other devices too once a storage is linked, so only do this on a fresh install.
3. **Open the menu (☰ at the top right) and tap Tutorial for this page** on any page for a short tutorial of that page: it highlights each box with a line about what it does, and “Other pages…” on its first card opens any other page's tutorial. The rest of the menu: **Install Vdex** (until it's installed), **Text size and theme** (Display & accessibility) and **Storage, sync & backups**, with a line at the top that says how syncing is going and the version of Vdex at the bottom. A dot on ☰ tells you about your cloud storage without opening the menu: amber while it is saving or when it needs you (sync is paused, or it wants you to sign in again), red when a save failed, and no dot when all is well. **🔒** sits beside ☰ once a PIN is set, and **🔍** on the same row searches everything you keep in Vdex (see *Find anything: the search*, below).

### What's on the Rolodex

The spinning drum at the top is how you move between sections: **swipe it** up or down, or tap **▲ ▼**.

| Card | What it's for |
|------|---------------|
| **Bookings** | Your calendar of shoots. Each booking holds the client, times, address, contacts, crew, kit, venue, notes, hours, its call sheet, and its pre/post-production checklist. |
| **Crew** | The freelancers you trust: role, phone, email, a link to their work, and which of your bookings they're on. A contact card someone shared from their Vdex goes in with **Add from a link**. |
| **Equipment** | Everything you own, with serial numbers, values, quantities and where each item is, and which shoots each item is booked on. The **Kits** tab saves sets of gear for the jobs you do most (an interview set, a car-shoot set…). **Import…** brings in the kit list you already keep in a spreadsheet or another app. **Share…** hands the list, a kit or chosen items to a workmate. |
| **Venues** | Places you shoot: map pin and directions, access and parking notes, nearest hospital, contact, and the rules (tripods, lighting, haze, drones… allowed, needs approval, or not allowed). |
| **Insurance** | Your policies, claims lines and renewal dates, with a warning if your kit is worth more than it's insured for. |
| **Me** | Your details and a QR code that hands your contact card, or your website, to anyone who scans it: they can save you to their phone, or add you to their own Vdex. |
| **Field** | Sun and golden hour, Polaris finder, white-balance reader, noise meter, range finder. |
| **TC Notes** | Shoot notes stamped with the camera's timecode, with your script alongside. Links your iPhone and a laptop so both can take notes. |
| **Settings** | Storage, security, calendar links, themes, defaults, backups, your project templates, and everything else. |

### Find anything: the search

The magnifier **🔍** at the top of every page, beside the menu, searches everything you keep in Vdex at once: bookings (the project, client, contacts, address, notes, shot list, deliverables, licences, people hired, kit rented and edit days), crew, equipment and kits (serial numbers and where things are, too), venues and their rules, insurance policies, quotes and invoices (by number, client or what is on a line), call sheets, stripboards, and timecode logs with their notes and scripts.

- **Type** a name, a place, a serial number, an invoice number or a word from your notes. Capitals and accents don't matter (“zoe” finds Zoë), a phone number can be typed without its spaces, and several words narrow it down: every one of them has to be there, in any order.
- **Results are grouped by page**, the best match first, with the words you typed marked and a line that shows where they were found. A match in a name or a title comes before a match in notes.
- **The chips** under the search box (All, Bookings, Crew, Equipment, Venues, Insurance, Money, Call sheets, TC Notes…) keep the results to one page, and each shows how many it has. Only pages that have something in them get a chip.
- **Tap a result** and Vdex opens that booking, person, item, venue, policy, log, quote, invoice or call sheet where it lives. A person, an item or a policy is shown on its own card in its list, outlined for a moment.
- It all happens on your iPhone: nothing you type is sent anywhere, and nothing about your searches is saved. What you last typed stays in the search box only until Vdex is closed or locked. Searching changes nothing, so it also works when Vdex is read-only, and while Vdex is locked there is nothing to search.

### Your crew and their roles

Crew → **+ Add** makes a card for someone you work with: name, role, phone, email, a day or hourly rate and a link to their work. The **Role** button opens a menu of the roles used in film, television and video work, more than 300 of them, folded into groups of similar jobs: Producing, Directing and ADs, Camera, Drone and specialist camera, Lighting, Grip, Sound, Data and DIT, Art department, Costume, Hair and make-up, Locations, Script and continuity, Casting, Talent and on-screen, Stunts and safety, Production support, Editing and colour, Sound post and music, VFX and motion graphics, Live, broadcast and events, Stills, Social and content, and General.

- **Tap a group** to open it, then **tap a role** to choose it. When the person already has a role, its group is open and the role is marked.
- **Search roles** at the top finds a role by any of its names. British names come first, and the American and short forms lead to the same job: "cinematographer" or "DP" finds **DOP**, "focus puller" finds **1st AC**, "makeup artist" finds **MUA**. Pick whichever name you use, and that is the one saved.
- **Not in the list?** Type it in the box at the end and tap **Use this role**. Once the card is saved, the role is kept under **My roles**, the first group, for next time, in your backups and on every device you sync. A role there that nobody has any more can be removed with **✕**.
- **Clear the role** takes it off again.

**+ Hire someone** on a booking page opens the same menu for the person hired for that job.

On the Crew page the chips under the search box filter **by group first**: tap **Camera** to see only your camera people, and when they have more than one role between them a second row narrows it to one (1st AC, Camera Operator…). Roles of your own are under **My roles**, and people with no role under **No role**. The search box finds people by name, by role, and by the group or another name of their role. The roles already on your cards stay exactly as they were.

### Add a booking

1. Bookings → **+ New** (or tap a date, then **+ New**).
2. Fill in the project, client, **job type** (this picks the right checklist: brand, corporate, event, wedding, music video, real estate, documentary, social media, short film, vertical video, series or feature film), dates (multi-day shoots have an end date), times and address.
3. Pick a **venue** if you've saved one: its address fills in and Vdex warns if your kit breaks the venue's rules.
4. Tick **crew** and **equipment**. Tick a **kit** to load a whole set of gear at once, then adjust. Items you own more than one of get a quantity.
5. Set the **approved break per day** if the production has one. The shoot-day clock uses it.

Vdex warns when the same piece of kit is needed on two overlapping jobs.

### Your own project templates

If you keep doing the same kind of job, make it a template once: Settings → *Work & billing* → **Project templates** → **+ New template**. A template is your own job type. It has a name and an icon, starts its checklists from one of the built-in types (edit them under *Workflow templates* afterwards), and holds what a new booking of that kind should begin with: usual start, finish and break, kits, equipment, crew, deliverables with their due dates counted from the shoot (say, the hero film 14 days after), a shot list, and the call sheet's schedule, dress code and instructions.

Then on the Bookings page tap **📐 From template** instead of + New: the booking form arrives filled in, you add the title and date, and on saving the deliverables, shots and call sheet lines are added too. Open any booking that went well and tap **📐 Save as template** to turn it into one; **↻ Apply template items** on a booking adds anything from its template that is still missing. Everything can still be changed on the booking itself.

### Run a shoot day

- On the day, open the booking: its **Hours** box has the clock. **● Start work** starts it (while it runs, a slim strip under the Rolodex shows the time and the same buttons on every page); **■ Stop work** logs the hours.
- **☕ Break** asks how long (15 min, 30 min, 1 hour, 2 hours), pauses the clock and counts the break down. **▶ Back to work** ends it when you're actually back. The bar keeps a running total against the break length approved for that production and warns when it's used up.
- **Notifications on an iPhone.** Turn them on under Settings → *Work & billing* → *Hours & breaks* (installed app only). Vdex counts the break only while it is on screen: the moment you switch to another app or lock the phone, iPhone pauses Vdex, so a "break's up" alert can't go off in the background. It shows the moment you reopen Vdex instead. For a reliable alarm on a long break, leave Vdex open with *Keep the screen on during breaks* switched on, or set a timer in the Clock app as well.
- **📋 Call sheets.** Bookings → **📋 Call sheets** lists every upcoming shoot and whether its sheet has gone out; the booking page has the same button for one shoot. Each sheet is built in the app from what Vdex already knows (venue address, access and parking, nearest hospital, crew and hired people with their call times and phones, kit, rentals, sunrise and sunset). Tap **✏️ Edit sheet** and change things directly on the document: general call, lunch, wrap, a schedule line by line, each person's call time, who is on set from the client, weather, dress code, the emergency contact, special instructions, and which sections to include; **Done** saves a new version. Then **🖨 Print** uses the iPhone's own print dialog (AirPrint, or save as PDF from there), **📄 Save PDF** makes a PDF file for the share sheet, **✉ Email crew** opens a message addressed to everyone on the shoot, **📤 Share** uses the share sheet, and **💾 Web page** makes a file that opens on any phone. The list shows ✅ once it has gone out and ⚠ if you changed it afterwards.
- **On the booking page**, under the summary: **Call sheet**, **Stripboard & shot list** and **Share with crew** in three boxes, the **Hours** box, the day's weather and sun, then one fold-down box for the **workflow, budget, quote and invoice**. Closed, its line shows the budget, the quote, the invoice and the stage at a glance; open it for the checklists (each stage has a **Check all** box; when every stage is done Vdex asks whether to archive the project) and the money. **People** holds the client's contacts, your crew and anyone you hired for the job (they go on the call sheet and the crew link like crew, and their cost into the budget); rented items are under **Equipment**. Lists longer than five fold behind **Show all**. **Edit days** on the booking (Edit → Edit days) show on the calendar in their own colour and go to your calendar feed; hours clocked on them count as editing.
- The **Stripboard & shot list** box on the booking page shows where the board stands and holds the shot list to tick off as you cover it. **Import shot list…** takes a text, CSV or Word file (or pasted text), one shot per line: scene headings such as *INT. KITCHEN - DAY* or *Scene 12* above the shots group them by scene and put any scene the board does not have yet on the stripboard, marked for a check (a shot list has no page counts, and often no time of day).

### Take timecode notes

1. TC Notes → **+ New log**. Choose the camera's frame rate and whether its timecode is **free run** (always counting) or **record run** (counts only while recording).
2. Tap **Sync**: type the timecode the camera shows and tap Sync at that instant, or point the iPhone at the camera's timecode display and tap **Scan**. The scanner downloads its text reader the first time, so do that once while you have a connection.
3. Type notes. Each one is stamped with the camera's timecode the moment you start typing. **⏺ Mark now** drops a stamp to fill in later.
4. In record run, tap **● Roll** and **■ Cut** with the camera; each take is logged with its length.
5. **Adding the script on an iPhone.** Tap **Script → Word document** and pick a `.docx` from the Files app (iCloud Drive, Google Drive and Dropbox all show up in Files). For a Google Doc, open it in the Docs app, tap ⋮ → *Share & export → Send a copy → Word* and save it to Files first, or select all, copy, and paste the text into Vdex. Then tap a line, or press and hold to select words, and **Mark** to pin a stamp to that part of the script.
6. **Export** shares the notes as text or a spreadsheet, to Mail, AirDrop, Files or anywhere else in the share sheet.

**Walkthroughs:** tap **📖 Guide** on the timecode page for a read-through of the whole job, from choosing the frame rate to exporting for the editor, with a **Walk me through it** button that runs *Timecode, start to finish*: about 25 steps on a practice log Vdex makes for you (synced, with notes and a sample script, so there is nothing to set up and no camera needed; delete it from ⚙ when you are done). **Tutorial for this page**, in the menu ☰, gives the short tour of the logs or of an open log, and the **?** inside the Sync, Link device, Add script and Export sheets their own steps. Quotes and invoices have the same: **🎓 New to quoting? Start here** on a booking's Money page (or the **📖 Quotes & invoices guide** under Tutorial for this page → Other pages…) is a read-through of the whole job, with a **Walk me through it** that runs *Quotes & invoices, start to finish* on a practice job Vdex makes: it sets the rates, quotes the job, sends and accepts the quote, invoices it from the quote and records a payment, with its own Q-PRACTICE and INV-PRACTICE numbers so your numbering is untouched. The last card removes the practice job.

**iPhone and laptop together:** tap **🔗 Link device** on both. One shows a code, the other scans it with its camera, then they swap. Both then show the same clock and the same notes, live, with no internet needed. They need to be on the same Wi‑Fi or one phone's hotspot. If the laptop has no camera, use **Copy code** and paste it on the other side.

### Stripboard: plan the shoot scene by scene

Open a booking and tap **🎬 Stripboard & shot list**. Tap **📄 Scenes from a script** and pick the script (a Word .docx, a Fountain or a plain text file, or paste the text): every scene heading (INT. or EXT., the set, then DAY or NIGHT) becomes a strip, with the characters who speak or are named in the action as its cast and an estimated page count in eighths. **＋ Scene** adds one by hand. The page counts are estimates from a standard screenplay layout: check them against your script.

Each strip is coloured as on a paper board: **white** interior day, **yellow** exterior day, **blue** interior night, **green** exterior night. It shows the scene number, the time of day, the set, what happens, the cast numbers (from **👥 Cast**, where you can renumber or rename people) and the pages. New strips wait under **Not scheduled yet**. Drag a strip by its **⠿** handle into a day, or focus the handle and use the up and down arrow keys, or tap the strip and pick its **Shooting day**. Each black **End of day** strip adds up that day's pages and scenes. **⚖ Spread over the days** shares the scenes out evenly by pages in their current order, **↕ Group unscheduled by location** puts scenes at the same set together, **＋ Banner** adds a line such as a company move, **＋ Day** adds a shooting day, and **🖨 Print** makes a one-line-per-scene schedule with the cast list. The call sheet gets a **Scenes** section for each day, and **👥 Share with crew** can include them (never a strip's notes).

### Quotes and invoices

Every booking has a **Money** block with a quote and an invoice. **From the booking** suggests the lines: shoot days at your day rate, each crew member at their own day or hourly rate (set on their card on the Crew page; an hourly rate is quoted for a standard day's hours per shoot day), the equipment package, and the deliverables. **＋ From the price list** adds the lines you tick from your own price list (a filming day, a drone day, colour grading, a music licence, rush delivery…), with day lines counted for each shoot day; edit anything, then **Mark as sent**. The invoice starts from the quote, from the hours the clock logged (shoot days, editing hours and overtime at your rates), or from the booking. Quotes and invoices print from the app, save as PDF, and email to the contacts on the booking (give a stakeholder an email address; on a booking added from a shared link they go to the person who shared it and the contacts you added yourself). Marking a quote sent or an invoice paid ticks the matching pipeline items. Bookings → **💰 Money** shows what is invoiced and unpaid, overdue, quoted and awaiting, and paid this year, and exports the invoices as a spreadsheet for the accountant. Rates, the price list, tax, numbering, payment terms and bank details live under Settings → *Work & billing* → **Quotes & invoices**. The price list starts from typical freelance rates for where you work, picked from your currency (the United States, Canada, the UK, Western Europe, or Australia and New Zealand; other currencies start with blank prices): starting points from published rate guides and surveys, not advice. Change any price, add your own lines, or start again from another region's rates under **🏷 Edit the price list**.

### Weather on the shoot day

Up to 16 days ahead, the booking page and the call sheet show the forecast for the shoot day at the venue, or at your saved location: conditions, temperature range, chance of rain and wind. **Add to call sheet** copies it onto the sheet so it prints. The forecast comes from Open-Meteo, a free service with no account; only the coordinates, rounded to about a kilometre, and the date are sent.

### Looking after the kit

Each item on the Equipment page has a **🔧 service log**: services, sensor and lens cleans, calibrations, checks, repairs and firmware updates, with dates, notes and costs. Give an item a service interval and a warranty date (Edit) and the page warns when a service is overdue or a warranty is about to end; Vdex also reminds you once in a while at start-up. The asset register export includes the warranty, firmware and last service.

Each item also has a **Where it is** field: a shelf, a case, the van, a person or the repair shop. It shows on the item's card (tap it to move the item in one go), in search, as 📍 filter chips, on the booking page's equipment list, and in the asset register. Places you have typed before are offered as you type.

### Bring in the kit list you already have

If your kit is already listed somewhere, you do not have to type it again. Equipment → **📥 Import…** takes:

- **a file**: CSV, or Excel (**.xlsx**). That covers your own spreadsheet, an export from another kit or inventory app, a rental house's list, an insurer's equipment schedule, and Vdex's own **asset register**. From Numbers or Google Sheets, export or download the sheet as CSV or Excel first. On an iPhone the file is chosen in the Files app, so save an email attachment to Files first.
- **pasted text**: rows copied out of a spreadsheet, or a plain list with one item on each line (`2x Aputure 600d` and `Sony FX6 (S/N 4512345)` are understood; a line ending in a colon, such as `Lenses:`, heads the lines under it).

Everything is read on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

1. **Check the columns.** Vdex shows each column with a few of its entries and what it takes it for: Name, Make or brand, Model, Category, Serial number, Quantity, Value, Where it is, Purchased, Warranty until, Service every, Firmware, Last service, Notes, or **Skip this column**. It goes by the heading and by what is in the column, so most files come out right first time; change any that is wrong. A Make and a Model join into the name. If the file has no row of headings, untick **The first row holds the headings**. A workbook with several sheets lets you choose the sheet.
2. **Read what would happen.** Under the columns are the first items as Vdex read them, the categories it changed (**Mics** becomes Audio, **Tripods** becomes Support; a category Vdex does not know is kept as you typed it), and a list of **rows to look at**: a row with no name, a value or date it could not read, text it had to shorten. Total lines and group headings are passed over, and a group heading such as **LENSES** gives the rows under it their category.
3. **Items you already have** are found by their **serial number** (capitals, spaces and hyphens do not matter); an item with no serial number is found by its name. For each one, or for all of them at once, choose **Skip it**, **Update mine with these details** (nothing of yours is blanked, and its service log stays) or **Add anyway**. Unless you choose otherwise they are skipped.
4. **Add.** The line above the button says exactly what will happen, for example *Adds 42, updates 3, skips 5*. Nothing is added before you tap that button.

Afterwards the new items are outlined on the Equipment page and a bar at the bottom offers **Undo** for a short while; after that, **Undo that import** is at the bottom of the Import sheet until you close Vdex or import something else. Undo removes what the import added and puts back what it updated, except items you have changed or put on a booking or in a kit since.

Good to know:

- Values are read with or without a currency sign, with either kind of thousands and decimal mark (`£1,299.00`, `1.299,00 €`), and `n/a` or a dash counts as empty. A quantity that is missing is 1 for a new item, and leaves your own quantity as it is when you update an item you already have. Dates are read in the usual forms; for a date such as 03/04/2024 Vdex looks at the rest of the column to tell the day from the month, and otherwise goes by your device's setting, so glance at the dates in the preview.
- The value Vdex keeps is the **replacement value of one item**. When a file has several money columns it takes the replacement value first, then a plain value or price, then what you paid; a column of line totals is left out.
- A formula in a cell is never worked out: Vdex keeps what the cell showed when the file was saved, as plain text.
- An old **.xls** file cannot be read: open it and save it as .xlsx or CSV. A file over 20 MB, or the rows after the first 10,000, are not read: import a big list in parts.
- Importing needs no licence. Like every change, it is not possible while Vdex is read-only.

### Keep track of a project

- Every booking has a **stage**: Enquiry → Quoted → Confirmed → Prepped → Shot → In post → Delivered → Archived. The strip on the booking page shows where it is; tap a stage to move it.
- Each stage has a **checklist** that depends on the job type. Tick things off, add your own items, and when a stage is complete a **Next** button moves the project on. Items with an arrow open the tool they're about (call sheet, kit, media log, hours…).
- **🗂 Pipeline** on the Bookings page shows every project by stage, with its progress and next deadline.
- Change the checklists for each job type under Settings → *Work & billing* → *Workflow templates*.
- **Budget** on the booking page: what the job will cost you, one line at a time, each in a category you pick from a list (crew, cast and talent, equipment hire, locations and permits, travel, accommodation, catering, art and wardrobe, hair and make-up, post-production, music and licences, insurance and more). Put in the estimate, then the actual cost once you know it. **Fill from booking** adds your crew and the people you hired at their day or hourly rates, your rentals and your licences. If the job has a quote, the budget shows what is left for you after costs. Only you see the budget: it never goes into a quote, an invoice or a crew link. **Export CSV** saves it for a spreadsheet.
- **👥 Share with crew** on the booking page makes a link to a read-only page for your cast and crew: the dates, call times, schedule, location, who is on, the scenes for each day from the stripboard, the shot list, the kit and the deliverables, whichever you tick. Prices, rates, budgets, quotes, invoices and your booking notes are never included. The whole page is inside the link, so there is no server and nothing is stored online. Crew can add the shoot to their calendar or print it, and crew who use Vdex can add it to their own Bookings (see *Share with workmates* below). If plans change, send a new link: an old one keeps showing what it had.

### Share with workmates, and add what they share

Three things can be handed to someone else as a link or a QR code. Everything travels inside the link itself, so there is no server and nothing is stored online, and anyone who has the link can read what is in it. Each link opens a page anyone can read, with an **＋ Add to my Vdex** button for people who use Vdex. A sent link cannot be withdrawn, and Vdex cannot confirm who made one. A booking's link never needs a licence. Your contact card's link or code and an equipment link can be made during the trial or with a licence; once the trial is over they need one (on the Me page, the Phone contacts and Website codes still work). Opening a link never needs a licence.

- **A booking.** **👥 Share with crew** on the booking page (above). On the crew's page, **Add to my Vdex** puts the shoot into their own Bookings: the dates and times, the place (as a venue of its own, with access, parking, power, the nearest hospital and the rules), the call sheet with its schedule, the scenes on a stripboard, the shot list and the deliverables. The crew you shared become the people on that booking, each with their call time, not entries on their Crew list; you and the client's contacts become its contacts; your equipment list goes into its notes, to read. Never any prices. The booking says *Shared by* you, and they can edit it like any other.
- **Your contact card.** The QR code on the **Me** page opens a small page with your details. Whoever scans it can tap **Save to my contacts (.vcf)** or **Add to my Vdex**, which puts you on their Crew list with your name, role, phone, email and website. On the Me page, **Phone contacts** switches the code to the plain contact card that a phone's camera saves by itself, with no page in between, and **Website** to your site. **My card's link…** gives the same link to copy, share or email instead of showing the code.
- **Equipment.** Equipment → **📤 Share…**: choose the **whole list** (with your kits), **a kit**, or **pick items**, then tap **Make the link**. It is a one-off copy, and **serial numbers and values go with it**, with each item's category, quantity, where it is kept, purchase and warranty dates, service interval, firmware and notes. The service log and your bookings do not. Your workmate can read and print the list, and **Add to my Vdex** copies the items into their own Equipment; a shared kit is made again as a kit.

A QR code is offered when the link is short enough to scan. A long one (a big equipment list, a booking with many scenes) still goes by **Copy link**, **Share…** or **Email**.

**Adding what someone shared.** Tap **Add to my Vdex** on the page. Vdex opens (after your PIN, if it is locked) and lists exactly what would be added; nothing is added until you tap **Add**. Adding the same thing again later **updates** what was added before instead of making a copy: a booking takes the new times, crew, schedule and shots and keeps your own stage and checklists, budget, quote, invoice, hours and anything you changed or added by hand (the sheet lists what is replaced and what stays); a contact keeps your rate and your notes, and a card that only shares a phone number, a name or an email address with someone on your Crew list is shown beside them, to add as a new person or to update them. With equipment, an item whose **serial number** you already have is listed as a duplicate, and for each one you choose **Skip it**, **Update mine** or **Add anyway**. With cloud storage linked, Vdex reads your cloud copy before it shows what a link holds, so a booking or a list you added on another of your devices is found on this one too. If the storage cannot be read at that moment (there is no connection, or it needs you to sign in again, as Google Drive does about an hour after the last sign-in), Vdex cannot know of it and offers to add; when it knows that it could not sync, the sheet says so. You then have two copies after the next sync, with nothing lost, and can delete the one you do not want. Adding needs no licence. While Vdex is read-only (after the trial, without a licence) it shows what a link holds but cannot add it.

**Add from a link**, on the Bookings, Crew and Equipment pages, is for a link that cannot open your copy of Vdex by itself: paste the link, or tap **Scan a QR code** and point the camera at the code on the other person's screen. On an iPhone a link opens in Safari, which keeps a separate copy from the installed app, so for the installed app copy the link first and use **Add from a link** there.

### After the shoot

- **Media & backups** on the booking page: one row per card; tick as it's copied to drive 1, drive 2, offsite, and verified. A card can only be marked *cleared* once it's on two drives.
- **Deliverables**: what the client gets, with due dates (they appear in your calendar), status and links.
- **Music & stock licences**: keep the licence numbers with the project.
- **✂ Start edit session** runs the clock for post days: on any day that isn't a shoot day, the Hours box near the top of the booking offers it in place of **Start work**, and the hours count as editing. **📊 Hours & statistics** shows totals, hours per week, hours by project and overtime, and shares a spreadsheet for invoicing.

### Calendar on an iPhone

The Google Calendar and Outlook options open those apps or sites ready-filled. **📤 Share calendar event** and **✉ Email** send the details to anyone. On a Mac, Calendar → *File → New Calendar Subscription* does the same and reaches the iPhone through iCloud.

4. **The phone's own calendar** (App Store app only). Bookings → **📆 Calendar & email** → *Two-way sync* → **This phone's calendar** → **Connect**, and allow calendar access when the phone asks. Vdex makes a "Vdex bookings" calendar in the Calendar app (in your iCloud calendars too, if the phone's calendars are in iCloud) and keeps it in step both ways, with no sign-in. On a booking, **📆 Add to calendar** → **This phone's calendar** opens the phone's own new-event screen, filled in, for a one-off copy.

### Field tools on an iPhone

Set your location first (Field → **Use GPS**, or Settings → *Field tool defaults*), then:

- **Sun** — sunrise, sunset, golden and blue hour, the sun's direction, and a slider to see any time of day. Aim the phone to find the sun (allow Motion & Orientation when asked).
- **Polaris** — where it is tonight, for aligning timelapses and star trackers (northern hemisphere).
- **WB** — a rough colour temperature from the camera. iPhones auto-correct colour, so treat it as a guide; a grey card and your camera's own white balance are the reference.
- **Noise** — a sound level estimate from the microphone. Calibrate it once against a real meter (Settings → *Field tool defaults*).
- **Range** — **AR measure isn't available on iPhones** (Safari can't do AR in a web app yet). Use the **tilt method**: enter the camera height, aim at where the subject meets the ground, and it works out the distance; aim at the top for the subject's height. About ±10% on level ground.

### Lock it: PIN, Face ID and Touch ID

1. Vdex asked for the PIN before it could be used (4 to 20 digits); **Change PIN…** under Settings → **Security** sets a new one. The lock screen shows a dot for each digit, in two rows when the PIN has more than ten, and opens by itself at the last digit. If your PIN was set while PINs stopped at 8 digits, Vdex asks once, after you next unlock, whether to **Set a new PIN** or **Keep my current PIN**; either way it does not ask again.
2. Switch on **Unlock with fingerprint, face or Windows Hello** if the switch appears. On an iPhone that is Face ID or Touch ID, and it works in Safari and in the installed app on iOS 16.4 or later (not yet in the App Store app, which opens with the PIN). If the switch doesn't appear, your iPhone or browser doesn't offer it to web apps, and the PIN is the way in.
3. Choose **Lock when you leave the app**: straight away, after 1, 5 or 15 minutes, or only when Vdex is closed. The 🔒 button in the top bar, beside the menu, locks it right now, for instance before handing the phone to someone.

What the lock does: the screen can't be used until the PIN or Face ID opens it, and everything Vdex stores on the phone (bookings, contacts, kit serials, insurance details, cloud sign-ins) is kept **encrypted** (AES-256) whenever Vdex is locked, in the background or closed. The PIN is never stored, only a key derived from it, so after five wrong tries the lock makes you wait, and you can choose to have the phone's Vdex data **erased after 10 wrong PINs** (only sensible with a cloud storage linked or a recent backup).

**If you forget the PIN** it cannot be recovered: tap *Forgot PIN?* on the lock screen, erase this phone's Vdex data, then link your cloud storage or import a backup and everything comes back. That is why the next section matters.

### Never lose your data

A stolen, lost or broken phone should cost you a phone, not your business records. Keep at least one copy somewhere else:

- **Link a cloud storage** (Google Drive, iCloud, Dropbox, OneDrive or WebDAV). Besides syncing, Vdex writes a dated backup next to its database once a day after a change: one file per weekday and one per month. Settings → *Storage, sync & backups* → **Restore an automatic cloud backup…** brings any of them back, which also undoes a mistake from earlier in the week. A restore replaces what is on this device and, at their next sync, on your other devices: what was added since that backup goes with it. A file given to **Import backup…** that holds only some of the lists (a backup from an early version, say) replaces only those, and the question before it is taken counts what will be deleted and names what stays.
- **Export a backup** now and then (Settings → *Storage, sync & backups*). The **Encrypted backup…** option protects the file with a passphrase, so it can sit in email or a shared drive safely. Both kinds import on any device.
- Settings shows the **last off-device copy** and Vdex reminds you, at most every few days, when there has been none for a week.
- **Equipment → Asset register (CSV)** (with a licence) exports everything you own with serial numbers and values. Keep a copy with your insurer and at home: it is what the police and the insurer ask for when kit goes missing. Equipment → **Import…** reads it back in, on this device or another.
- **If the phone is stolen:** with a PIN set, the Vdex data on it is encrypted. Change the password of the cloud account you linked, install Vdex on the new phone, link the same storage or import a backup, and set a new PIN.

### iPhone questions and answers

**I installed it and my bookings vanished.**
They're still in Safari: the installed app and the Safari tab are separate copies. In Safari, Settings → *Storage, sync & backups* → **Export backup** (with a licence), then **Import backup** in the installed app.

**My data disappeared after a few weeks.**
Safari clears website data it hasn't seen for a while. Use the installed app, link a cloud storage, and export a backup now and then (with a licence).

**The Install button only shows instructions.**
That's how iPhones work: web apps are added through Safari's Share button, not a download. The other way is the Vdex app on the App Store, which needs no installing step.

**I can't turn notifications on.**
Only the installed app can show them, on iOS 16.4 or later. Open Vdex from the Home Screen icon and try again. In the App Store app, allow them when the phone asks; if you refused once, iPhone **Settings → Vdex → Notifications**.

**The camera, microphone or GPS won't start.**
They need the installed app or a secure (https) address, iOS 16.4 or later, and your permission when asked. If you refused once, see *Let Vdex use the camera…* above.

**Google keeps asking me to sign in again.**
That's Google's rule for apps like this: a sign-in lasts about an hour. Tap **Sign in again** or **Sync now**; if you are still signed in to Google, the window closes by itself. Microsoft asks about once a day; Dropbox stays connected. Nothing is lost meanwhile: changes wait on the device.

**My iPhone and laptop won't link.**
Put both on the same Wi‑Fi or on the iPhone's hotspot (no internet needed). Some office and hotel networks keep devices apart; a hotspot always works. If one side can't scan, copy the code and paste it.

**Will I get a break notification if the phone is locked?**
In the App Store app, yes: the phone itself keeps the time, so the alert arrives with Vdex closed or the phone locked, with **Back to work** and **5 more minutes** on it. In the Safari-installed app, not while it's locked or in another app: iPhone pauses web apps straight away, and the alert appears when you reopen Vdex. Keep Vdex on screen with *Keep the screen on during breaks*, or use the Clock app's timer too.

**Can clients log in to review or approve?**
No. Vdex is your side of the job: you record their feedback and send them the documents.

**I deleted something by mistake.**
Import your last backup. If a cloud storage is linked, the deletion has already synced, so a backup is the way back.

**Does it update by itself?**
Yes. Whenever a new version is published, Vdex fetches it in the background the next time you open it and a bar at the bottom offers to reload; the next launch uses it anyway. Settings → *General* → *About & Licence* shows the version and has **Check for updates**. The App Store app shows the same website, so it is updated the same way; the App Store only updates the app around it now and then.

**Can I start over?**
Settings → *Storage, sync & backups* → **Erase all data on this device**. It asks twice, unlinks any storage, removes the PIN (Vdex asks for a new one at the next start), and leaves the cloud copy alone.

**I forgot my PIN.**
It can't be recovered. Tap *Forgot PIN?* on the lock screen to erase this phone's Vdex data, then link your storage or import a backup.

**Is my data encrypted?**
On the phone, yes once a PIN is set: whenever Vdex is locked, in the background or closed. The copy in your cloud storage is protected by that account's own sign-in, and backup files are plain unless you choose the encrypted kind.

### Getting paid

Vdex has no payment processor in the middle, and no fees. Instead, every invoice carries the ways you already get paid, and a link the client can open.

1. **Tell Vdex how you get paid**, once: Settings → *Work & billing* → *Quotes & invoices* → Getting paid → **+ Add a way to pay**, as many as you like: PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, a Stripe or Square payment link, Wise, Revolut, Monzo, Interac e-Transfer, UPI, a bank account, any other payment page, or plain instructions (cheque, cash, crypto…). Edit or remove them any time, and pick the currency for the links.
2. **Every invoice then carries Pay buttons** for the balance due, your bank details, a QR code that European banking apps scan to fill in the transfer (when the account is an IBAN), and the invoice number as the reference. They are on the printed invoice, in the PDF, in the email and on the client's page.
3. **Send the client a link.** On the invoice (or the quote), tap **Link for the client**. The whole document is packed inside the link, so nothing is stored online. Copy it, share it, email it, or let the client scan the QR code. It opens as a web page with the Pay buttons, Print and Save PDF.
4. **Record payments** as they arrive with **Record a payment**: the amount proposed is the balance, so a full payment is one tap. Part payments show as "part paid" with the balance; when the balance reaches zero the invoice is marked paid and the pipeline is ticked. The Pay buttons always ask for what is still owed.
5. **Chase gently.** An unpaid invoice past its due date shows as overdue, Vdex mentions it once a day when you open the app, and **Send a reminder** writes the email for you, with the balance, the date and the link.

The Money overview (Bookings → Money) adds up what is invoiced and unpaid, what is overdue, what is quoted, and what you received this year; the CSV for your accountant has the paid amount and the balance per invoice.

### Find anything: the search

The magnifier **🔍** at the top of every page, beside the menu, searches everything you keep in Vdex at once: bookings (the project, client, contacts, address, notes, shot list, deliverables, licences, people hired, kit rented and edit days), crew, equipment and kits (serial numbers and where things are, too), venues and their rules, insurance policies, quotes and invoices (by number, client or what is on a line), call sheets, stripboards, and timecode logs with their notes and scripts.

- **Type** a name, a place, a serial number, an invoice number or a word from your notes. Capitals and accents don't matter (“zoe” finds Zoë), a phone number can be typed without its spaces, and several words narrow it down: every one of them has to be there, in any order.
- **Results are grouped by page**, the best match first, with the words you typed marked and a line that shows where they were found. A match in a name or a title comes before a match in notes.
- **The chips** under the search box (All, Bookings, Crew, Equipment, Venues, Insurance, Money, Call sheets, TC Notes…) keep the results to one page, and each shows how many it has. Only pages that have something in them get a chip.
- **Tap a result** and Vdex opens that booking, person, item, venue, policy, log, quote, invoice or call sheet where it lives. A person, an item or a policy is shown on its own card in its list, outlined for a moment.
- It all happens on your phone: nothing you type is sent anywhere, and nothing about your searches is saved. What you last typed stays in the search box only until Vdex is closed or locked. Searching changes nothing, so it also works when Vdex is read-only, and while Vdex is locked there is nothing to search.

### Add a booking

1. Bookings → **+ New** (or tap a date, then **+ New**).

### Run a shoot day

- On the day, open the booking: its **Hours** box has the clock. **● Start work** starts it (while it runs, a slim strip under the Rolodex shows the time and the same buttons on every page); **■ Stop work** logs the hours.
- **☕ Break** asks how long (15 min, 30 min, 1 hour, 2 hours), pauses the clock and counts the break down. **▶ Back to work** ends it when you're actually back. The bar keeps a running total against the break length approved for that production and warns when it's used up.
- **Notifications on Android.** Turn them on under Settings → *Work & billing* → *Hours & breaks*. When a break is up, Android shows a notification with **▶ Back to work** and **5 more min** buttons, even while you're in another app, plus a two-minute warning. Android keeps Vdex counting in the background for a few minutes and may then pause it to save battery, in which case the alert arrives when you open Vdex again. For long breaks, switch on *Keep the screen on during breaks*, or stop Android pausing Chrome (phone **Settings → Apps → Chrome → Battery → Unrestricted**).
- **📋 Call sheets.** Bookings → **📋 Call sheets** lists every upcoming shoot and whether its sheet has gone out; the booking page has the same button for one shoot. ### Bring in the kit list you already have

If your kit is already listed somewhere, you do not have to type it again. Equipment → **📥 Import…** takes:

- **a file**: CSV, or Excel (**.xlsx**). That covers your own spreadsheet, an export from another kit or inventory app, a rental house's list, an insurer's equipment schedule, and Vdex's own **asset register**. From Numbers or Google Sheets, export or download the sheet as CSV or Excel first. The phone's file chooser opens: pick it from Downloads, Drive or wherever it is.
- **pasted text**: rows copied out of a spreadsheet, or a plain list with one item on each line (`2x Aputure 600d` and `Sony FX6 (S/N 4512345)` are understood; a line ending in a colon, such as `Lenses:`, heads the lines under it).

Everything is read on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

1. **Check the columns.** Vdex shows each column with a few of its entries and what it takes it for: Name, Make or brand, Model, Category, Serial number, Quantity, Value, Where it is, Purchased, Warranty until, Service every, Firmware, Last service, Notes, or **Skip this column**. It goes by the heading and by what is in the column, so most files come out right first time; change any that is wrong. A Make and a Model join into the name. If the file has no row of headings, untick **The first row holds the headings**. A workbook with several sheets lets you choose the sheet.
2. **Read what would happen.** Under the columns are the first items as Vdex read them, the categories it changed (**Mics** becomes Audio, **Tripods** becomes Support; a category Vdex does not know is kept as you typed it), and a list of **rows to look at**: a row with no name, a value or date it could not read, text it had to shorten. Total lines and group headings are passed over, and a group heading such as **LENSES** gives the rows under it their category.
3. **Items you already have** are found by their **serial number** (capitals, spaces and hyphens do not matter); an item with no serial number is found by its name. For each one, or for all of them at once, choose **Skip it**, **Update mine with these details** (nothing of yours is blanked, and its service log stays) or **Add anyway**. Unless you choose otherwise they are skipped.
4. **Add.** The line above the button says exactly what will happen, for example *Adds 42, updates 3, skips 5*. Nothing is added before you tap that button.

Afterwards the new items are outlined on the Equipment page and a bar at the bottom offers **Undo** for a short while; after that, **Undo that import** is at the bottom of the Import sheet until you close Vdex or import something else. Undo removes what the import added and puts back what it updated, except items you have changed or put on a booking or in a kit since.

Good to know:

- Values are read with or without a currency sign, with either kind of thousands and decimal mark (`£1,299.00`, `1.299,00 €`), and `n/a` or a dash counts as empty. A quantity that is missing is 1 for a new item, and leaves your own quantity as it is when you update an item you already have. Dates are read in the usual forms; for a date such as 03/04/2024 Vdex looks at the rest of the column to tell the day from the month, and otherwise goes by your device's setting, so glance at the dates in the preview.
- The value Vdex keeps is the **replacement value of one item**. When a file has several money columns it takes the replacement value first, then a plain value or price, then what you paid; a column of line totals is left out.
- A formula in a cell is never worked out: Vdex keeps what the cell showed when the file was saved, as plain text.
- An old **.xls** file cannot be read: open it and save it as .xlsx or CSV. A file over 20 MB, or the rows after the first 10,000, are not read: import a big list in parts.
- Importing needs no licence. Like every change, it is not possible while Vdex is read-only.

### Field tools on Android

### Lock it: PIN, fingerprint and face

1. Vdex asked for the PIN before it could be used (4 to 20 digits); **Change PIN…** under Settings → **Security** sets a new one. The lock screen shows a dot for each digit, in two rows when the PIN has more than ten, and opens by itself at the last digit. If your PIN was set while PINs stopped at 8 digits, Vdex asks once, after you next unlock, whether to **Set a new PIN** or **Keep my current PIN**; either way it does not ask again.
2. Switch on **Unlock with fingerprint, face or Windows Hello** if the switch appears. On Android that is the fingerprint, face or screen-lock check Chrome already uses. If the switch doesn't appear, your phone or browser doesn't offer it to web apps, and the PIN is the way in.
3. Choose **Lock when you leave the app**: straight away, after 1, 5 or 15 minutes, or only when Vdex is closed. The 🔒 button in the top bar, beside the menu, locks it right now, for instance before handing the phone to someone.

What the lock does: the screen can't be used until the PIN or your fingerprint opens it, and everything Vdex stores on the phone (bookings, contacts, kit serials, insurance details, cloud sign-ins) is kept **encrypted** (AES-256) whenever Vdex is locked, in the background or closed. The PIN is never stored, only a key derived from it, so after five wrong tries the lock makes you wait, and you can choose to have the phone's Vdex data **erased after 10 wrong PINs** (only sensible with a cloud storage linked or a recent backup).

**If you forget the PIN** it cannot be recovered: tap *Forgot PIN?* on the lock screen, erase this phone's Vdex data, then link your cloud storage or import a backup and everything comes back. That is why the next section matters.

### Never lose your data

A stolen, lost or broken phone should cost you a phone, not your business records. Keep at least one copy somewhere else:

- **Link a cloud storage** (Google Drive, iCloud, Dropbox, OneDrive or WebDAV). Besides syncing, Vdex writes a dated backup next to its database once a day after a change: one file per weekday and one per month. Settings → *Storage, sync & backups* → **Restore an automatic cloud backup…** brings any of them back, which also undoes a mistake from earlier in the week. A restore replaces what is on this device and, at their next sync, on your other devices: what was added since that backup goes with it. A file given to **Import backup…** that holds only some of the lists (a backup from an early version, say) replaces only those, and the question before it is taken counts what will be deleted and names what stays.
- **Export a backup** now and then (Settings → *Storage, sync & backups*). The **Encrypted backup…** option protects the file with a passphrase, so it can sit in email or a shared drive safely. Both kinds import on any device.
- Settings shows the **last off-device copy** and Vdex reminds you, at most every few days, when there has been none for a week.
- **Equipment → Asset register (CSV)** (with a licence) exports everything you own with serial numbers and values. Keep a copy with your insurer and at home: it is what the police and the insurer ask for when kit goes missing. Equipment → **Import…** reads it back in, on this device or another.
- **If the phone is stolen:** with a PIN set, the Vdex data on it is encrypted. Change the password of the cloud account you linked, install Vdex on the new phone, link the same storage or import a backup, and set a new PIN.

### Android questions and answers

**There's no Install button.**
Either Vdex is already installed (open it from your app list), or you're in a browser that can't install web apps. Open the address in Chrome.

**Notifications don't show.**
Allow them when Vdex asks, check the phone's notification settings for Chrome (or for Vdex under App info), and check Do Not Disturb. If alerts arrive late, Android has paused Vdex in the background: see *Run a shoot day* above.

**The camera, microphone or GPS won't start.**
They need a secure (https) address or the installed app, and your permission when asked. If you refused once, see *Let Vdex use the camera…* above.

**AR measure says it's not available.**
Your phone has no ARCore support, or you're not in Chrome. Use the tilt method instead.

**The sun pointer points the wrong way.**
Calibrate the compass by moving the phone in a figure of eight, and keep it away from magnets, cases with magnets and car mounts.

**Google keeps asking me to sign in again.**
That's Google's rule for apps like this: a sign-in lasts about an hour. Tap **Sign in again** or **Sync now**; if you are still signed in to Google, the window closes by itself. Microsoft asks about once a day; Dropbox stays connected. Nothing is lost meanwhile: changes wait on the device.

**My phone and laptop won't link.**
Put both on the same Wi‑Fi or on the phone's hotspot (no internet needed). Some office and hotel networks keep devices apart; a hotspot always works. If one side can't scan, copy the code and paste it.

**My bookings aren't on my other device.**
Each device starts with its own data. Link the same storage on both (menu ☰ → *Storage, sync & backups*), or export a backup on one and import it on the other (with a licence).

**Can clients log in to review or approve?**
No. Vdex is your side of the job: you record their feedback and send them the documents.

**I deleted something by mistake.**
Import your last backup. If a cloud storage is linked, the deletion has already synced, so a backup is the way back.

**Does it update by itself?**
Yes. Whenever a new version is published, Vdex fetches it in the background the next time you open it and a bar at the bottom offers to reload; the next launch uses it anyway. Settings → *General* → *About & Licence* shows the version and has **Check for updates**.

**Can I start over?**
Settings → *Storage, sync & backups* → **Erase all data on this device**. It asks twice, unlinks any storage, removes the PIN (Vdex asks for a new one at the next start), and leaves the cloud copy alone.

**I forgot my PIN.**
It can't be recovered. Tap *Forgot PIN?* on the lock screen to erase this phone's Vdex data, then link your storage or import a backup.

**Is my data encrypted?**
On the phone, yes once a PIN is set: whenever Vdex is locked, in the background or closed. The copy in your cloud storage is protected by that account's own sign-in, and backup files are plain unless you choose the encrypted kind.

## Using Vdex on a computer

### The easy way: a one-click installer

Open https://vdex.crowmade.app/download.html, download the file for your computer, and open it:

| Computer | File | Then |
|---|---|---|
| **Windows** | `Vdex-Setup.exe` | Double-click it. Vdex installs in a few seconds, opens, and gets a Start menu and desktop shortcut. While Vdex is new, Windows may show *Windows protected your PC*: click **More info**, check the publisher, then **Run anyway**. |
| **Mac** | `Vdex.dmg` | Open it and drag Vdex into Applications. If macOS says it cannot check the app, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**. |
| **Linux** | `Vdex.AppImage` | Make it executable (right-click → Properties → *Allow executing as program*, or `chmod +x`), then double-click it. |

**The licence.** Vdex is free to try for 30 days, on the website, on each computer and in the Android and iPhone apps. After that it needs a licence: **Buy a licence** in Settings → *General* → *About & Licence* (20 US dollars once plus the tax where you live, for up to 3 of your devices; in the UK, the EU and Australia the button shows the whole price with tax, and the download page lists them all; in the iPhone app the purchase goes through the App Store at the price it shows and works on every iPhone and iPad of your Apple Account without using any of the 3 places, **Restore purchases** brings it back after a reinstall or on another iPhone or iPad, **Add your email** puts an email address on it for signing in on the website and in the Android app, and **Copy the code for the desktop app** gives the code for a computer). **Delete my licence records**, under About & Licence while you are signed in, removes what the licence server keeps about you; your licence stays, and signing in again or restoring the purchase brings it back (so does a computer that still has your licence code, at its next weekly check: use **Release this computer** there first). On the website and in the Android and iPhone apps, press **Sign in** and use the email address you bought with: Vdex emails you a 6-digit code to type in. In the desktop app, paste the code from your email under **Enter licence code**. Keeping a copy in cloud storage, and saving backup and spreadsheet files and a calendar file of all your bookings, come with the licence (during the trial too); a storage linked before is paused until then. PDFs, printing and links for clients and crew never need a licence. Vdex activates it online once, and checks about once a week that the computer is still on the code. A code entered with no internet works for editing for 14 days, once, while it waits; cloud storage, spreadsheets and backup files start when the computer has been added online. Without a code once the trial is over, Vdex is read-only: everything opens and prints, and quote, invoice and crew links can still be sent, but nothing can be changed, imported or added, so no new quotes or invoices. To move a licence to a new computer, use **Release this computer** on the old one, or pick which computer to move it from when you activate it on the new one (up to 3 moves a month); the licence then no longer covers the computer it was moved from. If a licence you paid for ends, **Export backup** keeps working for 30 days, so you can save your information. Desktop installs that were in use before licences went on sale stay free.

The installed app opens in its own window, works offline, and keeps its data on your user account on that computer. It also has an extra storage option, **A file in a synced folder** (menu ☰ → *Storage, sync & backups* → *More ways to keep a copy*), which keeps the database as a file inside iCloud Drive (Mac), OneDrive, Google Drive for desktop, Dropbox or any synced folder with no sign-in. Phones can't use that option, so for phone-and-computer use choose Google Drive or iCloud (or Dropbox, OneDrive or WebDAV), and link the same one everywhere.

**Good to know about the desktop app**

- It and the browser version keep separate data. Pick one, or link the same cloud storage on both and they merge.
- There is no GPS in a computer, so set your location by hand: Field → **Enter**, or Settings → *Field tool defaults*. The sun, Polaris and call-sheet times then work as usual.
- Break notifications pop up like any other app's. They have no buttons; clicking one brings Vdex to the front.
- Backups, call sheets and exports save through a normal Save dialog instead of a share sheet.
- The PIN lock works the same way (every computer has a PIN too, 4 to 20 digits; Settings → *Security* changes it), with Windows Hello or Touch ID where the browser offers it. On the lock screen you can type the PIN on the keyboard, or paste it. It locks when the window goes to the background or when you press 🔒.
- **Updates:** the desktop app checks download.crowmade.app for a new version when it starts and every few hours. Windows, Linux and a signed Mac download it in the background and install it on the next restart (Settings → *General* → *About & Licence* shows progress and has **Restart to update**). On an unsigned Mac, Settings → *General* → *About & Licence* shows the new version with a download link instead, because Apple only allows silent updates for code-signed apps. The browser version updates like the phone apps do.
- **Search from the keyboard:** press **/** (when you are not typing in a box) or **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) on any page to open the search, the **↑ ↓** arrow keys to move through the results, **Enter** to open the one that is marked (with none marked, Enter marks the first) and **Esc** to close. In a wide window the search is a box in the top bar rather than a magnifier.
- To link a phone: the computer shows the code, the phone scans it and shows a reply; scan the reply with the webcam, or copy and paste it.
- A link someone shared with you (a booking, a contact card, an equipment list) opens in your web browser, not in the desktop app. To add it to the desktop app, copy the link and use **Add from a link** on the Bookings, Crew or Equipment page, or scan its QR code with the webcam. Links you make in the desktop app open on vdex.crowmade.app.
- Everything else, from bookings to timecode notes, works as described in the phone guides; the [Android guide](#android-guide) is the closer match.

### Or use it in a browser

Everything in the phone guides works the same on a computer in a browser, with no installer at all. In Chrome or Edge open Vdex's menu (**☰** at the top right) and tap **Install Vdex** (or use the install icon at the right of the address bar) to give it a window and an icon; Safari on a Mac has *File → Add to Dock*; Firefox and Chromebooks just use it in a tab. Chrome and Edge also offer the *file in a synced folder* storage option. With a wide window, timecode notes and the script sit side by side, and the Rolodex turns with the **arrow keys** or the mouse wheel.
