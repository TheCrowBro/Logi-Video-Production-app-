Anyone who has a quote, invoice or crew link, a link to your contact card or to equipment you shared, or your calendar feed link, can read what is in it, including names and contact details, and for equipment the serial numbers and values. A crew link always carries the dates, times, schedule, location and venue details, the emergency contact and your own contact details, plus whatever is ticked when you make it. At first everything is ticked except the client's contacts, including the client's name and the crew's names, roles, call times, phone numbers and email addresses. A crew link never carries prices, rates, budgets, quotes, invoices, kit values or your booking notes. A call sheet is different: it carries what you put in it and, unless you untick them under Include while editing it, the booking notes and the client's contacts too, and Email crew sends all of it. Switching the feed off in Vdex does not stop a feed link you already shared from working; delete the feed file (vdex-bookings.ics) in your storage to do that.

An equipment link carries, for each item you chose, its name, category, serial number, where it is kept, quantity, value, purchase and warranty dates, service interval, firmware and notes, with your name and business, your currency symbol and the kits you included; never an item's service log or your bookings.
