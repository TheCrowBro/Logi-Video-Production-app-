You decide who receives what you send from Vdex. Anyone who has a quote, invoice or crew link, or your calendar feed link, can read what is in it, including names and contact details. A crew link always carries the dates, times, schedule, location and venue details, the emergency contact and your own contact details, plus what you ticked when you made it, such as the crew's names, roles, call times, phone numbers and email addresses. It never carries prices, rates, budgets, quotes, invoices, kit values or your booking notes. A calendar feed lists every booking that is not cancelled, with client and crew names, contacts' phone numbers and booking notes, so share its link only with people who may see all of that. A sent link cannot be withdrawn or changed: if you correct a document, send a new link and tell the client the old one is out of date. Links open through Vdex's website and may stop working if Vdex changes or stops, so keep the PDF as your record. Switching the feed off in Vdex does not stop a feed link you already shared from working; delete the feed file (vdex-bookings.ics) in your storage to do that.
