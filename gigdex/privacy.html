Privacy

Your information stays on your device. Gigdex keeps your bookings, people, songs, money and everything else in this browser's storage on this phone or computer. There is no Gigdex account and no Gigdex server that receives your data.

When something leaves your device

  • Backups go only where you save or share the file.
  • Share links (a day sheet for the band, an invoice for a client) carry the document inside the link itself, after the # sign, which browsers never send to a server. Anyone you give the link to can read that document, so send it only to the people it is for.
  • Maps: a venue's map preview loads tiles from OpenStreetMap, and “Find from address” asks OpenStreetMap's address search, which sees that address and your internet address. Directions buttons open Google Maps or Apple Maps.
  • The microphone (tuner and sound meter) is used only while that tool is open, on the device, and nothing is recorded or sent.

Removing your data

Settings → Erase this device removes everything Gigdex keeps in this browser. Uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data in your browser does the same.
